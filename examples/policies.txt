# Backups

Production databases are backed up daily and backups are stored in a second region. Backups are encrypted. Restore tests are performed once a year.

# Recovery objectives

The recovery time objective for the core platform is 8 hours and the recovery point objective is 24 hours.

# Process

We maintain a documented incident response plan with defined roles and an on-call rota. The plan is exercised through a tabletop exercise every year.

# Notification

Customers are notified of a confirmed personal data breach without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after we become aware of it. Regulators are notified within 72 hours where the law requires it.

=== information-security.md ===
---
title: Information Security Policy
reviewed: 2026-06-15
---
# Authentication

All employees and contractors must use multi-factor authentication for e-mail, the VPN and every administrative console. MFA is enforced by the identity provider and exceptions are not permitted. Single sign-on through SAML is available for all customer-facing applications.

# Encryption

Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are managed in a cloud key management service with automatic yearly rotation. Laptops use full disk encryption.

# Vulnerability management

Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 14 days and high ones within 30 days. External penetration tests are performed once a year by an independent firm and the findings are tracked to closure. We do not currently run red team exercises.

# Security operations

Logs from production systems are collected in a central SIEM and retained for 12 months. A 24x7 security operations center is planned for 2027 and is not yet in place. Alerts are reviewed during business hours today.

# People

All staff complete security awareness training when they join and every year after that, with quarterly phishing simulations.

# Delegado de protección de datos

La empresa ha designado un delegado de protección de datos (DPO) que depende directamente de la dirección y es el punto de contacto con la autoridad de control.

# Proveedores y subencargados

Los subencargados del tratamiento se evalúan antes de su contratación y se formalizan mediante contrato conforme al artículo 28 del RGPD. La lista de subencargados está disponible para los clientes bajo petición.

# Conservación

Los datos de los clientes se conservan mientras dure el contrato y se suprimen en un plazo máximo de 90 días tras su finalización.

# Certificaciones

La certificación ISO 27001 está en curso y se prevé obtenerla durante 2027. Actualmente no disponemos del certificado.
